## 🧩 About

**Lies of P helpers** is a curated list of Lies of P open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, stat readouts, menu panels
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — inventory notes, weapon catalog, config presets
- **Research Notes** — boss layouts and build notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Combat Helpers
- Stamina Notes — track stamina usage during study runs
- Hit Window Viewer — visualize timing windows for study
- Boss Pattern Notes — record and review boss attack layouts
- Playback Speed — adjust practice playback for study (0.1x to 3x)

### 🎨 Catalog & Loadout
- Weapon Catalog — browse weapons, blades, and handles
- P-Organ Notes — organize organ upgrade paths
- Legion Arm Notes — catalog arm modules and effects
- Costume Notes — organize outfit cosmetics

### 🏋️ Practice Layout
- Practice Segment Notes — segment study on any area
- Respec Notes — track stat respec plans in single-player
- Menu Panel Notes — organize overlay tabs and hotkeys
